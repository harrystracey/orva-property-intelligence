About Al Tamr

Al Tamr is a high-end residential building which is a part of the Shoreline Apartments complex in Palm Jumeirah. This residence offers individuals with an exclusive lifestyle through beautifully designed accommodations and modern amenities. Apartments in Al Tamr range from 1 to 3 bedroom apartments and also include 4-bedroom penthouses. Each residential unit has been constructed to give residents breath-taking views of the waterfront, as well as the main road. Amenities in Al Tamr consist of a laundry room, waste disposal system, Jacuzzi, children’s play area and landscaped garden. There are also steam/sauna rooms, barbeque area, daycare centre, first-aid medical centre and restaurant facilities. For fitness, there is a fully-equipped gymnasium and swimming pool.

Al Tamr is located in the Shoreline Apartments development. This complex comprises 20 residential buildings that are each lined towards the East part of Palm Jumeirah. These residential skyscrapers offer a luxurious way of living and multiple amenities.

Since Al Tamr Shoreline is located in a popular location, it is close to multiple landmarks, restaurants, attractions and transportation points. Residents can use the public transportation located around the building to reach destinations such as Nakheel Mall, Mall of the Emirates and Dubai Marina Mall. They can also visit attractions such as Lost Chambers Aquarium, The Palm, Dubai Marina Walk and Skydive Dubai. Other ways of transit are using very reliable boat/yacht services, which are located just a few minutes away.

Al Tamr is located in Palm Jumeirah, which is a popular community in Dubai. This self-proclaimed wonder of the world is an island that is shaped like a palm tree. It is home to several high-end developments, retail outlets, shopping malls, entertainment points and much more.

  • Close to retail stores, restaurants and supermarkets

Facilities in Al Tamr

Parking

There are multiple spaces for parking in Al Tamr in its underground levels. Each apartment unit is provided with at least one slot for parking.

Elevators

There are fully-functional elevators in Al Tamr that are available for the leisure of residents.

Security, Central Air-Conditioning and Maintenance Services 

Al Tamr building Palm Jumeirah features an advanced 24-hour security system that includes CCTV cameras, smoke detectors, skilled security guards and a monitoring room. Maintenance services consist of waste disposal and repair facilities. DEWA is responsible for providing all the utilities in this tower.

Event Space

There is a conference room in Al Tamr that can be used as an event space.

Gym and Fitness Facilities 

There is a gym in Al Tamr, as well as two squash courts, one walking/jogging trail, fitness and health club. There is also a swimming pool.

Lifestyle Amenities

Al Tamr offers individuals with a luxurious lifestyle that is filled with multiple amenities. Each floor has been designed with high-quality materials, as well as architecture. Amenities in this building include rich green landscapes, a gymnasium, swimming pool, infinity pool, children’s play area, spa, steam room and a beautiful lobby. Al Tamr also provides 24-hour security that is provided by a skilled team of security guards. There is also underground parking.

Concierge and Guest Services 

This residence has front desk services.

Pet-friendly

Al Tamr is a pet-friendly building.

Safety Features

Al Tamr provides safety features that include multiple smoke detectors, as well as fire extinguishers on each floor.

Apartment Types in Al Tamr

Al Tamr is a high-end residential project that consists of 1-bedroom, 2-bedroom and 3-bedroom apartments, along with 4-bedroom penthouses that offer a variety of amenities to residents. Residents with these accommodations can be eligible to get inclusive access to multiple fitness and leisure facilities in this building.

The various apartment types in Al Tamr are described as below:

1-Bedroom Apartments in Al Tamr

1-bedroom apartments in Al Tamr offer residents with a large room that includes a built-in closet and wide window. There is also a living room/dining area, kitchen space, one or two bathrooms, one powder room and a walk-in balcony. This accommodation measures at 1,144 sq.ft. on average.

2-Bedroom Apartments in Al Tamr

2-bedroom apartments in Al Tamr can come with a balcony, living room, kitchen and powder room for guests. In total there are two or three bathrooms and two separate rooms. This apartment unit measures at an average area size of 1,583 sq.ft.

3-Bedroom Apartments in Al Tamr

3-bedroom apartments in Al Tamr feature three rooms which each have their own closet. In total, there are two to three bathrooms, one powder room, one living room/dining area, walk-in balcony and kitchen. This unit measures at an average size of 2,159 sq.ft.

4-Bedroom Penthouses in Al Tamr

4-bedroom penthouses in Al Tamr feature a large space for residents. With four separate rooms, each of them with built-in closets and large windows. Aside from that, there is a large walk-in balcony with seating arrangements. There is a large living room that has a floor-to-ceiling window, which is wide enough to give residents a 180-degree of the skyline. Aside from that, there is a kitchen which is well-equipped with modern construction. This accommodation area measures from 4,843 sq.ft. to 4,844 sq.ft.

Transportation near Al Tamr 

Residents can find plenty of public transport stations. These modes of transportation near Al Tamr comprise trams, buses and metro trains.

Bus Stations near Al Tamr

Bus stations near Al Tamr include:

  • Knowledge Village (4 minutes away)
  • Dubai Knowledge Village Conference Centre 2 (6 minutes away)
  • Dubai Knowledge Village Conference Centre (7 minutes away)

Other close by bus stations include Royal Mirage Hotel 2 2, International Advertising Association Building station and The Westin Dubai Mina Seyahi Beach Hotel 1 station.

Metro Stations near Al Tamr

Metro Stations near Al Tamr include:

  • Mall of the Emirates (7 minutes away)
  • First Abu Dhabi Bank (8 minutes away)
  • Mashreq Metro Station 1 (9 minutes away)

Other metro stations close to Al Tamr include Dubai Internet City Metro Station 2, Nakheel and DMCC Metro Station 1.

Tram Stations near Al Tamr

Tram Stations near Al Tamr include:

  • Palm Jumeirah Monorail (3 minutes away)
  • Palm Jumeirah 1 (7 minutes away)
  • Palm Jumeirah (8 minutes away)

Other tram stations close to Al Tamr include Knowledge Village 2, Media City Tram Station 2 and Al Sufouh.

Restaurants near Al Tamr

Restaurants on the Palm Dubai near Al Tamr include:

  • Shakespeare and Co. (3 minutes away)
  • Bidi Bondi (4 minutes away)
  • Cinque (6 minutes away)

Shakespeare and Co. is a brilliantly designed cafe, which dedicates itself to serving French, Italian, Middle Eastern and Arabian cuisine. This cafe is popular for serving mouth-watering items and desserts. Launched in 2001 in Dubai, this cafe boasts delicious recipes, such as granola bowl, hummus trio and rainbow pad thai.

Bidi Bondi is an Italian fast-food restaurant in Palm Jumeirah. This restaurant is popular for serving a wide range of mouth-watering recipes with beautiful views of Palm Jumeirah. Some of the customer favourites include the tuscan pizza, taco salad, the lamb fillet and malva pudding.

Previously known as Quattro Passi, Cinque is a fine-dining restaurant in Palm Jumeirah that specialises in Italian cuisine. Aside from the gorgeous picturesque ambience, favourites in Cinque include their wine, pasta and tiramisu. Its dessert menu includes delicious nutella soufflé.

Cinque also offers a full bar option to customers which offers a wide range of liquor, cocktails and finger-food. Customers can choose between sitting indoors or outdoors.

Supermarkets near Al Tamr

Supermarkets near Al Tamr include:

  • Spinneys (3 minutes away)
  • Choithrams (4 minutes away)
  • Allday Minimart (5 minutes away)

Individuals looking to stock up on their groceries can visit Spinneys, which is is a popular choice for getting essentials. This store sells a fresh variety of meat, vegetables, fruits, snacks, canned food and household supplies.

Allday Minimart is a popular grocery store that is known for serving a wide variety of fresh gourmet, perishables, canned items and household supplies.

Choithrams is another option that offers a wide variety of fresh vegetables, fruits, canned items, gourmet, seafood, snacks and household supplies.

Nearby Buildings

Some of the buildings near The Waves Tower A include:

Al Tamr is situated in a well-built community that offers beautiful scenic views. Its neighbours include Al Khushkar, Al Dabasa and Al Basri.

Major Landmarks

Major landmarks near Al Tamr include:

  • Nakheel Mall (5 minutes away)
  • The Pointe (9 minutes away)
  • The Lost Chambers Aquarium (11 minutes away)
  • Aquaventure Waterpark (12 minutes away)

The Pointe is a large entertainment and dining development in Palm Jumeirah. Constructed by Nakheel Properties, this project features over 100 seafront restaurants and shopping outlets.

Things to Consider

Located in a popular spot for tourists, Al Tamr is a skyscraper building that is surrounded by multiple conveniences, transit stops and landmarks. It offers multiple types of apartments at affordable prices. One thing to consider is that during peak hours there may be large crowds but that can be easily avoided if residents travel before or after peak hours.
