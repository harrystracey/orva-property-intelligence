About Al Nabat

Al Nabat is an 11-storey residential tower in Palm Jumeirah. It is a part of the Shoreline Apartments by Nakheel and offers 1, 2 and 3-bedroom apartments and 4-bedroom penthouses. Facilities include covered parking and 24-hour security along with fitness and leisure options. The tower was completed in 2007.

Palm Jumeirah is among the most popular residential vicinities in Dubai. It is known for its beautiful waterfront views, dining options and luxurious living standards. Divided into crescent, trunk and fronds, the community offers various apartments and villa projects along with hotels and leisure destinations.

Residents also get leisure options like beach access, clubhouses and a play area for children. The retail section features a restaurant and salons for men and women. Sheikh Zayed Road is nearby and public transport is also readily available.

In a Nutshell

Facilities in Al Nabat 

PARKING

Residents get covered parking in Al Nabat Palm Jumeirah.

ELEVATORS

There are multiple high-speed elevators in Al Nabat Shoreline Apartments.

SECURITY, CENTRAL AIR-CONDITIONING AND MAINTENANCE SERVICES

As part of a gated complex, the tower has a 24-hour security system with trained staff and a CCTV system. Empower provides district cooling services.

EVENT SPACE

The barbecue area can be used for gatherings and events.

GYM AND FITNESS FACILITIES 

There is a fully equipped gym in Al Nabat. Residents also get to use the swimming pool, jacuzzi, sauna and steam room. Please note that access to fitness facilities such as the gym comes with an extra fee payable to a third-party provider.

LIFESTYLE AMENITIES

Residents get various leisure options in Al Nabat including beach access, a barbecue area and exclusive clubhouses. The tower also offers a dedicated play area for children.

CONCIERGE AND GUEST SERVICES 

Concierge staff is present to welcome visitors and guests.

PET-FRIENDLY

It is a pet-friendly building.

Apartment Types in Al Nabat

Al Nabat Palm Jumeirah offers 1, 2 and 3-bedroom apartments along with 4-bedroom penthouses.

1-BEDROOM APARTMENTS IN AL NABAT 

1-bedroom apartments in Al Nabat include one or two bathrooms, a living/dining area, a kitchen and a balcony. The covered areas range from 1,136 sq. ft. to 1,509 sq. ft.

2-BEDROOM APARTMENTS IN AL NABAT

2-bedroom apartments in Al Nabat include three bathrooms, a living/dining area, a maid’s room, a kitchen and a balcony. These units cover areas of about 1,549 sq. ft. to 1,994 sq. ft.

3-BEDROOM APARTMENTS IN AL NABAT

3-bedroom apartments in Al Nabat include four bathrooms, a living/dining area, a maid’s room, a kitchen and a balcony. The covered areas range from 2,140 sq. ft. to 2,250 sq. ft.

4-BEDROOM PENTHOUSES IN AL NABAT

4-bedroom penthouses in Al Nabat include five bathrooms, a living/dining area, a maid’s room, a kitchen and a balcony.

NEARBY AMENITIES 

Transportation near Al Nabat 

It takes about 10 minutes to get to Sheikh Zayed Road. Residents with private vehicles can use it to connect with various important areas and destinations in Dubai. One can also rely on public transportation near Al Nabat as taxis, buses and metro are readily available.

BUS STATIONS NEAR AL NABAT

Knowledge Village Bus Stop is about an 8-minute drive away. It is serviced by buses 8 and N55.

METRO STATIONS NEAR AL NABAT

Dubai Internet City Metro Station is the nearest option to use Dubai Metro. It is about a 13-minute drive away.

Restaurants near Al Nabat

Riva Restaurant is present on the ground floor of the building. Residents can also dine at these restaurants near Al Nabat:

  • Little Neighbourhood Restaurant
  • Koko Bay
  • Blvd on One
  • Ella’s Eatery
  • The Legends Restaurant & Cafe
  • The Noodle House
  • Shake Shack
  • Itsu
  • Ibn AlBahr Seafood Restaurant
  • Rumba Dubai
  • The Locale

All these restaurants in Palm Jumeirah are reachable within 8 minutes. Cuisines available include Arabic, American, Chinese, Cuban and Lebanese.

Supermarkets near Al Nabat

Popular supermarkets near Al Nabat include:

  • Choithrams
  • Spinneys
  • Viva Supermarket

All these are among the top supermarkets in Palm Jumeirah. Choithrams and Spinneys are reachable within a 5-minute walk. One can get to Viva Supermarket within a 5-minute drive.

Schools and Nurseries near Al Nabat

  • Blossom Palm Jumeirah
  • Redwood Montessori
  • The International School of Choueifat (ISC)

Blossom Palm Jumeirah follows the EYFS curriculum whereas Redwood Montessori offers education based on the Montessori method of teaching. Both are reachable within 5 minutes and are counted among the top nurseries in Palm Jumeirah.

The International School of Choueifat (ISC) follows the SABIS Educational System and offers education from KG1 to Grade 12.

Nearby Buildings

  • Al Msalli
  • Al Haseer
  • Al Sarrood

All these residential towers are located close to Al Nabat Shoreline and offer a wide range of apartments for rent and sale.

Major Landmarks

  • Aquaventure Waterpark
  • Dubai Marina Mall
  • Emirates Golf Club

Aquaventure Waterpark is about 10 minutes away from Al Nabat. It is a popular leisure destination and offers various pools and thrilling rides to visitors.

Dubai Marina Mall offers a wide range of shopping, dining and entertainment options. It is located about 13 minutes away and is counted among the top malls in Dubai.

Emirates Golf Club offers top facilities for players and spectators along with dining options. The club is about 13 minutes away.

Things to Consider

Al Nabat offers comfortable living in a pleasant environment. Living in Palm Jumeirah ensures access to beaches and leisure destinations. Although there aren’t any bus or metro stations within walking distance, residents can easily find taxis within the community.
