About Al Sultana

Al Sultana is a mid-rise building on the trunk of Palm Jumeirah. The construction of the project started in 2004 and was completed in 2006. The estimated height of the building is 41 metres, and it has 10 floors above ground. Apartments in Al Sultana include 1, 2, 3 and 4-bedroom units. Residents enjoy easy access to restaurants, shopping malls, supermarkets, educational institutes and hospitals.

Amenities in Al Sultana, Palm Jumeirah, include a swimming pool, gymnasium, steam/sauna rooms, kids’ play area, barbeque pits, sports courts and Jacuzzi.

Set on the coast of the Arabian Sea, Palm Jumeirah is the largest manmade island on the planet. It is home to many luxury apartments, villas, townhouses and hotels. Among the residential projects is Shoreline Apartments, an apartment complex by Nakheel that features 20 mid-rise buildings. Al Sultana is a part of the Shoreline apartments in Palm Jumeirah.

Facilities in Al Sultana

Parking

There are dedicated spaces for parking in Al Sultana.

Elevators

There are multiple high-speed elevators in Al Sultana.

Security, Central Air-Conditioning and Maintenance Services 

Al Sultana, Dubai, is highly secure with state-of-the-art 24-hour CCTV security. Regular cleaning, waste disposal, housekeeping and maintenance services are available. An emergency generator is also present on-site. Utility services are provided by the Dubai Electricity and Water Authority (DEWA).

Event Space

Residents can organise events in the community centre. Residents can also use the swimming pool for weekend pool parties.

Gym and Fitness Facilities 

There is a fully equipped gym in Al Sultana along with separate swimming pools for children and adults. Residents also have access to sports facilities such as table tennis, golf club, tennis court, polo club, squash courts and water sports.

Lifestyle Amenities

Al Sultana has a kid’s play area, a daycare centre, and many retail and entertainment options. Residents can find Jacuzzi, sauna and steam room facilities. The BBQ area is there for perfect family time. Lawns and gardens allow you to spend some time outdoors.

Concierge and Guest Services 

The building offers 24-hour concierge and front desk services.

Pet-friendly

Al Sultana, Palm Jumeirah is pet-friendly.

Apartment Types in Al Sultana

Apartment types in Al Sultana include 1, 2, 3 and 4-bed units.

1-Bedroom Apartments in Al Sultana

1-bedroom apartments have a covered area of 1,144 sq. ft to 1,145 sq. ft. These units feature a separate room with walk-in closets and wardrobes, an attached bathroom and a powder room. Each unit has a modern fitted kitchen and spacious living room with dining space.

2-Bedroom Apartments in Al Sultana

2-bedroom apartments cover 1,991 sq. ft. to 1,993 sq. ft. Each unit has a maid’s room, a guest bathroom, a large living room and a fitted kitchen. A balcony is attached to these units providing wonderful views of the sea.

3-Bedroom Apartments in Al Sultana

The covered area for a 3-bed flat in Al Sultana ranges from 2,206 sq. ft. to 2,260 sq. ft. These units include three separate rooms with built-in wardrobes and attached bathrooms. There is a maid’s room with a maid’s bathroom, a fitted kitchen, a spacious living room and a balcony in these apartments.

4-Bedroom Penthouses in Al Sultana

4-bedroom penthouses span 5,250 sq. ft. On the ground floor, there is one separate room with a built-in cabinet and attached bathroom, living room and fitted kitchen. On the first floor, there are 3 separate bedrooms with attached bathrooms, a kitchen, large storage, and a sitting area.

NEARBY AMENITIES

Transportation near Al Sultana

As for transportation near Al Sultana, residents can find taxis and buses.

Bus Stations near Al Sultana

  • Royal Mirage Hotel 1 2 (7 minutes away)
  • Dubai Media City, Building 15 (8 minutes away)
  • Middle East Broadcasting Center (8 minutes away)

Other close bus stops include Knowledge Village Main Gate 2, Knowledge Village Main Gate 1 and Dubai Knowledge Village Conference Centre 2.

Metro Stations near Al Sultana

Dubai Metro stations nearby include DMCC Metro Station and Dubai Internet City Metro Station.

Restaurants near Al Sultana

There are plenty of restaurants near Al Sultana.

  • Bidi Bondi (2 minutes away)
  • Peaches & Cream Beach Restaurant (4 minutes away)
  • Wagamama (4 minutes away)

Bidi Bondi specialises in serving burgers and Italian food. It is situated on the ground floor of Al Manhal and can be reached within 2 minutes.

Peaches & Cream Beach Restaurant is known for serving Mediterranean cuisines. It offers a full bar, beach access with indoor and outdoor seating areas.

At the same driving distance, you can also find a Japanese restaurant called Wagamama. This restaurant serves traditional and modern Japanese and Asian cuisines. It is one of the best restaurants in Palm Jumeirah.

Supermarkets near Al Sultana

Supermarkets near Al Sultana include:

  • Allday Minimart (2 minutes away)
  • Choithrams (5 minutes away)
  • Spinneys (5 minutes away)

Allday Minimart is a grocery store that is popular for selling fresh gourmet, perishables, canned items and household supplies.

Choithrams is one of the best supermarkets in Dubai. This store offers a wide variety of fresh vegetables, fruits, canned items, gourmet, seafood, snacks and household supplies.

Schools and Nurseries Near Al Sultana

  • Blossom Nursery
  • iCademy Middle East
  • American School of Dubai

Blossom Nursery is an 11-minute walk from the building. Redwood Montessori and Asya's Nursery are within a 5-minute drive from Al Sultana Building.

iCademy Middle East is a private online school that follows the K-12 educational programme as per the US curriculum. The school is an 8-minute drive away from Al Sultana.

American School of Dubai is a non-profit community school that follows the US Curriculum. The school is a 12-minute drive from the building.

The Winchester School Jebel Ali is part of the GEMS Education group. It follows National Curriculum for England and is a 14-minute drive from the building.

Nearby Buildings

Some of the nearest apartment buildings include:

  • Jash Falqa, Shoreline 4 – residential building
  • Al Tamr, Shoreline 15 – residential building
  • Jash Hamad, Shoreline 18 – residential building
  • Abu Keibal, Shoreline 17 – residential building

These are accessible within 4-minute drive by car. Other buildings at a 4 to 5-minute drive include Al Basri, Shoreline 1, Al Dabas, Shoreline 2, Golden Mile 1, Golden Mile 2, Golden Mile 3 and Golden Mile 4.

Major Landmarks

Landmarks near Al Sultana include:

  • The Pointe
  • The Lost Chambers Aquarium 
  • Aquaventure Waterpark
  • Dolphin Bay

The Pointe is an iconic project by Nakheel which lies at a 8-minute drive away from Al Sultana Palm Jumeirah Dubai.

The Lost Chambers Aquarium is an Atlantis-themed aquarium featuring underwater tunnels housing marine life. It is located at an 8-minute drive by car. Aquaventure Waterpark is located on the ground of the famous Atlantis The Palm. It is the largest waterpark in Dubai attracting locals and tourists. It is accessible at a 10-minute drive by car.

You can also find Dolphin Bay and Sea Lion Point within an 11-minute drive from the building. Aquaventure Beach is a 10-minute drive away.

Things to Consider

Al Sultana is surrounded by multiple conveniences, transit stops and landmarks. Residents can enjoy a vibrant lifestyle in Palm Jumeirah. Lack of public transport, however, can be a problem at times.
