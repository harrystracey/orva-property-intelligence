───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 FEATURE 3: AI RENTAL QUERIES
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

In HLM chat, ask:

"Show me Fairmont owners with expiring leases"
  → Table with lease expiry dates + owner contacts

"What's the rental yield for 2-beds in Shoreline 12?"
  → Avg rent / Avg sale price × 100
  → Last 12 months rent, last 6 months sales

"Is unit 607 in Marina 3 currently rented?"
  → Active/Expired status, contract end date, annual rent

"Show rental history for Shoreline 12, Unit 607"
  → All contracts, renewal detection, tenant stability

"Tell me everything about Shoreline 12, Unit 607"
  → Owner contact + purchase history + rental status + yield

"Which buildings have the highest rental yields?"
  → Ranked list by yield %

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 KEY FORMULAS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Gross Rental Yield:
  (Average Annual Rent / Average Sale Price) × 100

Where:
  - Avg Annual Rent = Last 12 months of rental contracts
  - Avg Sale Price = Last 6 months of sales transactions
  - Typical Palm Jumeirah yields: 5-8% for apartments

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 HOT LEAD STRATEGY
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1. Go to Lease Expiry Dashboard
2. Set expiry window to 90 days
3. Filter to your target (e.g., "Shoreline" + "2 beds")
4. Export leases WITH owner contact (📞 ✅)
5. Call owners 60-90 days before expiry
6. Pitch: "Your lease is expiring, have you considered selling?"

Why it works:
  - Owners are actively making decisions at lease expiry
  - You have their phone number (vs cold calling)
  - You can calculate their equity gain + rental yield
